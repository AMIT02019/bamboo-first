A Year Of Honour & Pride

Yet another year of honour & pride for tribal sisters of Bamboo First as they celebrate rakshabandhan with navy officers at naval base INS Angre, Mumbai. They were accompanied by sisters from Mumbai who are part of this Keshav Srushti led noble cause for tribal upliftment.

Bamboo Rakhis For The Navy

They tied bamboo rakhis to navy officers & expressed gratitude. Company CEO Dilip Ghatal shared information about the life transforming bamboo project. Navy officers said this rakhi gives us strength while we are away from home in service to nation.

Gratitude

Grateful to Laksh Atha ji & Manoj Bubna ji for their consistent efforts to make this memorable celebration possible for consecutive 3 years.

OUR MENTORS

We are blessed to be in the lap of the Bharat mata where numerous great men have been born in every era and guided society towards the path of progress and development. Two such visionaries of contemporary times Former PM Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee ji and our current PM Shri Narendra Modi ji presented a unique vision for marginalized sections of the society like ours.

Our mentors HDFC Life, Cactus Communications Pvt Ltd, Young volunteers organisation, gem & jewellery national relief foundation and Keshav Srushti have been instrumental in driving this vision to change on ground, shaping us from an ordinary rural community to the present Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Co. Pvt. Ltd.

Our Mentors

Guiding Lights

Guided by Vision, Backed by Purpose

We are blessed to be in the lap of Bharat Mata where numerous visionaries have guided society towards progress, empowerment, and self-reliance. Two such towering figures of contemporary times — former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee ji and our current Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi ji — presented a transformative roadmap for tribal communities like ours.

Our foundational mentors Keshav Srushti, NABARD, HDFC Life, Cactus Communications Pvt Ltd, Young Volunteers Organisation, and Gem & Jewellery National Relief Foundation have been instrumental in turning this vision into tangible reality on the ground. They helped shape us from an ordinary rural community into the thriving Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited.

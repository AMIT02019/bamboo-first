Media & Press Highlights

23rd Nov 2023 • Palghar

'Bhoomi Poojan' Ceremony for CFC Construction at Balkapra Village

Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producers Company Ltd (VBUPCL) and Keshav Srushti conducted the sacred 'Bhoomi Poojan' ceremony of their Common Facility Centre (CFC) at Balkapra Village in Jawahar block.

This state-of-the-art facility is dedicated to empowering tribal communities by providing bamboo-centric skill training, modern processing, exhibition, and direct marketing for handicrafts curated by tribal women. The facility is situated within a dense plantation of 5,500 bamboo saplings across 15 acres of tribal land.

National Recognition • Episode 96

Featured in Hon'ble PM Narendra Modi's Mann Ki Baat

Hon. Prime Minister of Bharat Shri Narendra Modi mentioned our grassroots effort in the 96th edition of 'Mann Ki Baat' with a featured video of our tribal women artisans crafting bamboo articles in Vikramgad.

“Sustainability is the buzzword all over the world, and the true answer lies in being close to nature. 'Bamboo First' is providing effective solutions with bamboo as a medium to fight climate change and empower rural tribal communities with dignifying employment.”

International Milestone • Export Success

500 Bamboo Auto Rickshaws Exported to the UK

Showcasing precision craft and design ingenuity, our tribal women artisans successfully fulfilled an international order of 500 handcrafted miniature bamboo auto-rickshaws for clients in the United Kingdom.

This accomplishment proved that rural tribal artisans equipped with proper design mentoring can compete on global platforms with zero compromise on quality and environmental integrity.

Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited. Supported by NABARD & Keshav Srushti.

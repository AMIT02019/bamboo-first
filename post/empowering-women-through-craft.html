Empowering Women Through Craft

Transforming Rural Communities in Palghar

Bamboo handicrafts are more than just beautiful objects; they are a testament to the skill, artistry, and resilience of women in rural India. Through initiatives like Bamboo First and the Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited, these women are finding new avenues for economic independence and personal growth.

Historically, tribal women in Palghar had limited earning opportunities, often reliant on low-paying seasonal agriculture or migratory manual labour. By learning the delicate nuances of bamboo splitting, seasoning, shaping, and weaving, hundreds of women have become self-employed master artisans.

Beyond income generation, this craft fosters social empowerment. Women now actively participate in decision-making within their households and producer company boardrooms, reinvesting their earnings into their children's education, healthcare, and nutrition.

When you hold a Bamboo First creation, you are holding a piece of that journey — an artisan's dedication, her family's dignity, and a sustainable step towards rural India's self-reliance.

Bamboo and Women Empowerment

A Grassroots Movement for Self-Reliance

Bamboo First is more than just a brand; it's an economic movement rooted in rural Palghar empowering hundreds of tribal matriarchs. In many rural areas, women face systemic barriers to employment and financial autonomy.

Through dedicated training hubs and Common Facility Centres supported by Keshav Srushti and NABARD, women receive end-to-end training in raw bamboo harvesting, processing, product design, quality control, packaging, and commercial enterprise management.

Today, these artisans are not just workers — they are shareholders and board directors of the Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited. They own their company, set their production targets, and inspire younger generations of girls in their villages to aim high.

The success of Bamboo First exemplifies how ancient natural resources combined with modern mentorship can catalyze lasting social reform.

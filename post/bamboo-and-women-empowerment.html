Bamboo and Women Empowerment

Building Economic Self-Reliance in Rural Palghar

Bamboo First is more than just an artisan brand; it is an economic movement rooted in rural Palghar. By transferring ownership of the enterprise directly to tribal women shareholders, the Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited has disrupted traditional top-down development models.

Today, 90% of the company's 253+ founding shareholders are tribal women. These women are not merely factory workers or wage-earners; they are board directors, financial controllers, master trainers, and custodians of community enterprise.

This leadership model has transformed household dynamics across 25+ villages. Women who once faced seasonal distress migration now operate flourishing home workstations, earn consistent monthly revenues, and lead community development councils.

As India marches toward Atmanirbharta, the women of Vikramgad stand as proof that local sustainability and women-led entrepreneurship can spark nationwide transformation.

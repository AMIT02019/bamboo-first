Eco-Tourism by VBUPCL

Our Identity

Who we are?

Welcome to the world of sustainable tourism presented by “Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited” (VBUPCL). VBUPCL is a Producer Company supported by NABARD for bamboo craft artisans of Vikramgad block in Palghar district.

It is an extension of the social initiative “Project Green Gold” led by Keshav Srushti since 2019 for skilling & entrepreneurship development of tribal women. Employment, Empowerment & Environment has been the core of the project. Hundreds of tribal lives have been transformed since then & Bamboo Ecotourism has been an essential part of this successful journey.

Our Mission

What we do?

We aim to create sustainable entrepreneurship opportunities for tribal communities through bamboo, with a special focus on empowering women, all while prioritising the protection of the natural environment.

We have built a bridge to connect urban people with rural ways of sustainable living that strive to preserve nature, culture, art, values, and traditions of Bharat.

Experience Authenticity

What You Get?

Immerse yourself in a range of activities that connect you with the heart of village life. Witness various organic farm produce as per the season, enjoy the lush green nature during monsoons, and listen to captivating stories narrated by pristine water bodies.

Get hands-on experience in making bamboo crafts, authentic Warli art, and much more. Live a day without gadgets and embrace a more human experience. Bring your family, and choose cheerfulness over materialism.

Experience Eco Life First Hand

A full-day immersive itinerary curated to rejuvenate your soul and support tribal artisans.

1. Traditional Welcome

Warm welcome amid vibrant rangolis, tribal tarpa dance, chiming lezims, and a refreshing cup of local herbal black tea.

2. Bamboo Craft Workshop

Hands-on guided workshop where master tribal artisans teach you how to cut, shape, and weave bamboo into beautiful articles.

3. Organic Village Lunch

Savour your tastebuds with authentic rustic lunch cooked on earthen stoves using fresh seasonal organic farm produce.

4. Guided Nature & Farm Walk

Explore local bamboo plantations, river streams, freshwater ponds, biodiversity corridors, and heritage village landmarks.

5. Warli Art Workshop

Learn the geometric sacred folklore of authentic Warli painting directly from indigenous tribal Warli artists.

6. Folk Dance & Souvenirs

Join the rhythm in the joyous Gauri nritya folk dance and shop affordable handcrafted bamboo souvenirs directly from artisans.

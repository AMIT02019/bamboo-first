The Genesis

Our Story

Bamboo has long been a symbol of resilience, positivity, and strength — qualities that resonate deeply with humanity's journey. This remarkable plant, revered for its 1,500+ documented uses, has the potential to transform lives and entire rural communities, as envisioned by our visionary former Prime Minister, Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee.

In 2004, at the seventh World Bamboo Congress in New Delhi, he foresaw bamboo's incredible economic value, predicting its ability to lift millions out of poverty and contribute significantly to India's economy. His words planted the seeds of a movement that would take shape years later.

In 2014, another visionary leader, Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, reignited the call for Atmanirbharta (self-reliance). Meanwhile, our mentor organization, Keshav Srushti, was tirelessly working on rural development through initiatives like solar-powered irrigation under the Keshav Srushti Gram Vikas Yojana (KSGVY).

By 2017, KSGVY turned PM Vajpayee's prophetic vision into action with Project Green Gold. This initiative trained underprivileged tribal women from five villages in Wada taluka in the art of bamboo handicrafts, offering them a sustainable livelihood beyond seasonal farming. After three years of rigorous development, the community launched its first signature product — the handcrafted bamboo rakhi.

This newfound empowerment bore fruit even during the challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic. Villages in Palghar district delivered over 50,000 bamboo rakhis in 2020 and over 8,000 bamboo lanterns (kandeels) in subsequent years. The product range expanded to 100+ creations, spanning festive, devotional, home, lifestyle, and gifting categories. A global milestone came when the community successfully fulfilled an international export order of 500 bamboo auto rickshaws for the UK, showcasing their world-class design capabilities.

In 2023, on the auspicious occasion of Gudi Padwa, the Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited was formally registered — India's first tribal women-led rural agri-startup. Shortly after, Bamboo First was launched, introducing the world to our unique e-commerce platform of bamboo products crafted with care and purpose.

Today, Bamboo First stands as a testament to the strength of community, the power of sustainability, and the spirit of tribal women who are building a self-reliant future. Through every product we create, we celebrate a journey rooted in tradition and growing towards empowerment.

Our 5-Year Journey

In just five years, Bamboo First has grown from a visionary rural initiative into a thriving nationwide movement redefining eco-conscious living. From stationery and kitchen essentials to home decor and cultural exports, we have inspired thousands to make greener choices.

We partner with local farmers, support bamboo agro-forestry, and raise awareness about environmental sustainability. We thank every customer, mentor, and partner for walking this path with us.

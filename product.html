The Artisan's Touch & Heritage

Every piece is meticulously handcrafted by tribal women artisans belonging to the Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited. Harvested from local mature bamboo thickets in the rural forests of Palghar, each bamboo culm is seasoned, precision-cut, and manually lathed using time-honored tribal craftsmanship techniques passed down through generations.

By eliminating synthetic industrial resins and plastics, we preserve the natural grain, warmth, and strength of sustainable Indian bamboo while ensuring ethical wages and dignity of labor for our rural producers.

Empowering Tribal Women in 25+ Palghar Villages

Bamboo First operates in direct partnership with NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development) and Keshav Srushti to foster women's micro-enterprises and village self-reliance.

Care & Longevity Guide

• Cleaning: Wipe gently with a soft damp cotton cloth. Avoid submerging in standing water or commercial dishwashers.

• Drying: Ensure the product is thoroughly air-dried in a shaded, well-ventilated spot if exposed to rain or moisture.

• Longevity Polish: Apply a light dab of pure coconut oil, linseed oil, or mineral oil once every 3-4 months to restore its glowing natural bamboo luster.

• End of Life: 100% compostable and biodegradable at the end of its multi-year lifespan, returning cleanly to Mother Earth.

Eco-Conscious Packaging & Safe Transit

• Zero Plastic Cushioning: We wrap every bamboo creation in biodegradable honeycomb kraft paper and natural jute cord.

• Crush-Tested Outer Box: Packed in double-walled corrugated shipping cartons designed to endure long transit without bending.

• Dispatch Timeline: Handcrafted orders are carefully inspected and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours directly from Vikramgad, Palghar.

Project Green Gold

By Keshav Srushti

Keshav Srushti's Gram Vikas Yojana is actively involved in making villages “Self-Sustainable” through its broad initiatives under verticals like Education, Health, Agriculture and Industry.

In year 2017, once Indian Forest Act was amended to remove “Bamboo” from the Tree category and bring it under the Grass category, it unlocked huge potential for Tribal communities for sustainable living through bamboo cultivation and harvesting.

Keshav Srushti built a business model around the bamboo crop that is Environment Friendly, Self-Sustaining, and Empowering for village communities—especially Tribal Women. This initiative was named “Project Green Gold”.

Why Choose Bamboo?

Renewable Resource: Bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants on earth, harvestable every 3 years without harming root systems or deforesting ecosystems.

Eco-Friendly & Carbon Neutral: Bamboo releases 35% more oxygen and absorbs up to 12 tons of carbon dioxide per hectare per year compared to standard timber trees.

Versatility and Durability: With tensile strength surpassing mild steel, bamboo crafts are resilient, stylish, and built for lasting lifetime daily use.

Biodegradable and Non-Toxic: Naturally pest-resistant and free from chemical treatments, ensuring healthy homes and zero long-term plastic pollution.

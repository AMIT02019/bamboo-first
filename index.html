Project Green Gold

By Keshav Srushti

Keshav Srushti's Gram Vikas Yojana is actively involved in making villages “Self-Sustainable” through its broad initiatives under verticals like Education, Health, Agriculture and Industry.

In year 2017, once Indian Forest Act was amended to remove “Bamboo” from Tree category and bring it under Grass category, it opened a huge potential for Tribal communities for sustainable living through Bamboo cultivation and Harvesting.

Keshav Srushti in its pursuit to develop sustainable living for Tribal communities in Vikramgad Taluka (District: Palghar), found an innovative idea to build a business model around Bamboo crop which would be Environment Friendly, Self Sustaining and Empowering the village communities especially the Tribal Women. This initiative was named “Project Green Gold”.

Under this initiative, Keshav Srushti started training of 25 Women in Tetawali Village in Jan 2019 to make Handicraft related products from Bamboo. In view of the encouraging response and the hardwork of Keshav Srushti's Team, a company was registered by name “Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Ltd” which is 100% owned and managed by Tribal Artisans.

As of March 2024, the company has 253 shareholders (mainly Tribal Women) and giving employment to more than 800+ Tribal Women.

Why Choose Bamboo?

Renewable Resource: Bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants in the world, making it a highly sustainable and renewable resource. It can be harvested regularly without depleting the ecosystem.

Eco-Friendly and Sustainable: Bamboo helps combat climate change by absorbing large amounts of carbon dioxide. It also requires less water compared to other crops, making it an environmentally friendly choice.

Versatility and Durability: Bamboo is incredibly versatile and can be used in a wide range of products, including furniture, flooring, textiles, and kitchenware. It is also highly durable, often stronger than wood.

Biodegradable and Non-Toxic: Bamboo products are biodegradable, reducing waste and environmental impact. Additionally, bamboo is naturally free from pesticides and harmful chemicals, making it a safe and non-toxic option for various uses.

Another Milestone

Keshav Srushti achieved another milestone in it's bamboo skill & enterprise development journey as it inaugurated the 'Mahila Swavalamban Kendre' (Common Facility Centre) at the Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park on 26th Sep 2026.

Built Under CSR Initiative 'Roshni'

The CFC is built under the CSR initiative 'Roshni' by renowned business group RR Kabel. Machines for the CFC are supported by NABARD.

Dignitaries Present

RR Kabel Chairman Shri. Rameshwarlal Kabra, Smt. Ratnidevi Kabra, MD Shri. Mahendra Kabra, RSS Mumbai Mahanagar Sanghachalak Shri. Suresh Bhageria, Keshav Srushti Margadarshak Shri. Bimal Kedia, AGM NABARD RO Shri. Khokan Dey, DDM NABARD Palghar Shri. Sudhanshu Ashwini, Project Officer & SDM Jawhar Shri. Sidharth Ramkumar (IAS) were the dignitaries present during inauguration ceremony who showered their wishes & blessings for future.
